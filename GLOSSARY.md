# Glossary

Shared vocabulary for this project, for humans and agents alike. Use these names in code, plans and conversation. Add terms as they come up, keep entries short, and sort each section alphabetically.

## Editor UI

- **Alt-drag (copy)**: holding `Alt` on a move drag (the body, the x/z arrows, the y arrow) copies instead of moving: the originals stay, and the copies follow the cursor with the selection highlight and a `· copy` label. `Alt` counts at release, so it can be pressed or released mid-drag. On the y arrow a copy snaps onto the originals' tops (a second floor). The copies become the selection. Scale handles and drawing keep `Alt` = from the center (from 05).
- **convert to free-form**: the inspector's button (Actions) when the selection has boxes or cylinders: each becomes a free-form with the same outline, in one step (`Convert box_3 to freeform_4`). The free-form gets a new ID and takes its original's place, keeping its name, group, kind, color, y and height. A smooth cylinder becomes 4 smooth points (still a true circle or oval), and the free-forms become the selection. The agent converts with `update_nodes { id, type: "freeform" }` (from 06).
- **copy, cut and paste**: `⌘C` / `⌘X` / `⌘V` on the selection, through the browser's copy, cut and paste events and the system clipboard, so they work across scenes, tabs and reloads. The clipboard holds `{ "dungeonDesigner": "nodes", "nodes": [...] }` (the selection with whole subtrees), and anything else is ignored. Paste gives fresh IDs, keeps names and nesting, lands centered in the view (offset snapped to 0.5 m, each box keeping its y), goes into the entered group if there is one, and selects what it pasted. Cut is one step (`Cut box_3`). Off while typing in a field or in the picker (from 05).
- **box tool**: `B`, drags boxes on the ground with the kind and color from the contextual bar. `Shift` makes the footprint square, `Alt` draws from the center, and `Cmd/Ctrl` turns off snap. Drawing never changes the selection, and the tool stays Box. Replaces the separate room and volume tools (from 03). From 07B it draws on the **surface** under the press (see **drawing on surfaces**).
- **camera**: the 3D perspective camera with a narrow (30°) field of view, so perspective stays mild (from 02). Its pitch is fixed, it orbits the focus point with yaw only, and zoom moves it toward the focus point.
- **canvas**: the full-window 3D drawing surface (the viewport). It fills the whole browser window.
- **compass rose**: the small round compass at the bottom right of the view. Its red needle and N point where north (-z) is on screen, turning with the camera's yaw. Clicking it turns the view north-up (yaw 0) (from 06).
- **contextual bar**: the bar that floats just above the tool-bar, with only the kind toggle (room, volume, hole) and the color swatches: for the drawing tools' next shape, or in the Select tool for the selection (kind for a single closed shape, color for all selected shapes). Every other field moved to the **inspector** in 07B (before, it also held sides, walls, taper and bevel, tilt, line and ramp controls, the mirror buttons and the read-only numbers).
- **cylinder tool**: `C`, drags a cylinder's footprint exactly like the box tool (`Shift` makes it a circle, `Alt` from the center, `Cmd/Ctrl` no snap), with the kind and color from the contextual bar and the **sides** from the inspector (from 06).
- **draft**: the live preview of a box while you drag its footprint, drawn lighter, with a `W × D m` label next to the cursor. `Esc` cancels it.
- **drawing on surfaces**: the Box, Cylinder and Pen tools start a shape on the flat surface under the press (from 07B): a volume's top, a room's floor or the top of its walls, whichever is nearest along the view, else the ground. Only level faces count, so slopes (a taper, a bevel), tilted shapes, ramps and sides are passed through, and holes and lines never count. The rest of the drag stays on that level, the new shape's `y` is its height, and a label next to the cursor says where it lands (`on hall's floor · y 3`). The Line and Ramp tools always placed each point on the surface under the cursor (`surfaceUnder` in `pick.ts`).
- **edit points (point editing)**: the mode for reshaping a free-form. Enter it by double-clicking the free-form or with **Edit points** in the inspector. Points show as squares on its top face, and a selected point's handles as dots on stems. Drag points (`Shift`-click selects several, 0.5 m snap, `Cmd/Ctrl` free) or handles (the opposite one stays in line, `Alt` breaks it). Click an edge to add a point without changing the shape, double-click a point for corner ↔ smooth, and `Delete` removes points (at least 3 stay). Each edit is one step (`Reshape freeform_1`), and one that would cross itself shows red and isn't kept. `Esc`, **Done** or clicking outside leaves it (from 06).
- **entered group**: the group you've double-clicked into. Clicks, the marquee and `Cmd/Ctrl+A` then act on its children, and the inspector's header says `lobby › …`. `Esc` or clicking outside it leaves it (from 03). From 07B, what you draw there (Box, Cylinder, Pen, Line, Ramp) goes into it, like a paste.
- **error panel**: the red `N errors` pill at the top right, hidden while there are none. It opens a list of what went wrong in this tab: a render crash (the 3D view shows *Reload the view* instead of going blank), an uncaught error or rejection, an error from the server. Click an entry to see its stack. **Copy** copies the log and **Clear** empties it (from 06).
- **focus point**: the ground point under the center of the screen. The camera orbits it and pans move it (from 02).
- **graybox**: the look of the view: warm near-white matte materials with a 1 m tile texture, a sun with soft shadows and faint outlines, like a blocked-out game level.
- **grid**: the reference lines on the ground plane: a line every 1 m and a stronger one every 5 m, fading out away from the focus point. It's a measuring and snapping aid only and is never part of the scene data. The view bar's **grid** toggle hides it (from 07B).
- **hand tool**: `H`, navigation only: drag to pan and scroll to zoom toward the focus point. Holding `Space` switches to it temporarily from any tool (from 03; in 02 it also selected).
- **height handle**: the small yellow cube on the top center of a single selected box, part of the transform gizmo. Dragging it changes the box's height with the bottom fixed, in 0.05 m steps (`Cmd/Ctrl` for free), with a live `h … m` label. It replaces 02's height gizmo (the blue stem and cone) (from 03).
- **info-label**: the status bar docked along the bottom edge, full width, one line: the connection state, the cursor's ground position (x · z), the camera yaw, the room, volume and hole counts (the **Show holes** toggle was here in 07, and moved to the **view bar** in 07B), and the current tool's hint on the right (a short notice, in yellow, can replace the hint for a moment). The numbers are fixed-width so it never jitters. The 3D view ends above it (a small top-right box before 03, a floating row across the top from 03, the bottom bar from 05, when the project bar moved out of it).
- **isolate**: showing only one node and what's in it (from 07B), with the outliner row's isolate button or `I` (on the single selected node; `I` again, the view bar's ✕, or `Esc` with nothing selected shows everything). Isolating a group enters it, so what's drawn next goes in it. Anything new while isolated (drawn, pasted, the agent's) shows too. Hidden nodes can't be seen, picked, drawn on or snapped to, but holes outside the isolation still cut what shows. For this tab only, not an edit; the agent sees it as `view.isolated`.
- **inspector**: the floating panel with the fields of the selection, or of the drawing tool's next shape (from 07B). Its header names what it shows (`lobby (box_3)`, `3 selected`, `next box`, `lobby › box_3` in an entered group); it's dragged by the header (double-click puts it back top right), collapses to it, remembers both per viewer and stays on screen. Below the header: the read-only numbers, then sections: Shape (sides), Walls (thickness), Profile (taper, bevel), Tilt (pitch, roll, Reset tilt), Ramp (width, smooth or steps, base, Reverse), Line (thickness, dashes, arrows, Reverse) and Actions (mirror X / Z, Convert to free-form, Edit points). Sliders preview on the shapes while dragged and send one step on release; fields send on `Enter` or when left (`Inspector.tsx`).
- **lock (`locked`)**: a node marked `locked: true` (from 07B, with the outliner row's lock button or `update_nodes`): it and everything in it can't be clicked, hovered, marquee-selected or picked by `Cmd/Ctrl+A` in the view, but still count as surfaces to draw on and to snap to (draw on an island without grabbing it). The outliner still selects a locked node, and then it edits normally. Locking and unlocking are undo steps (`Lock box_3`). A human's aid: the agent may still change locked nodes, and its instructions ask it to leave them alone unless asked.
- **line tool**: `L`, draws a line: each click places a point on the surface under the cursor (a volume's top, a room's floor or wall top, else the ground; x/z snap to 0.5 m, `Cmd/Ctrl` no snap), click-drag pulls out handles for a curve, and a double-click or `Enter` finishes. `Backspace` removes the last point and `Esc` drops the line. The preview shows it in the contextual bar's style (color, black by default, thickness, dashes, arrows) (from 06).
- **marquee**: the rectangle you drag on empty ground with the Select tool to select every box it touches on screen (its exact outline, not its bounds), live as you drag. `Shift` adds to the selection and `Esc` restores the selection it started from (from 03).
- **origin axes**: a marker at the world origin, with +x as a red bar, +y as a green bar and +z as a blue bar (the gizmo's colors), for orientation while rotating.
- **outliner**: the collapsible tree panel on the left listing every node by name (or ID) with a room / volume / group icon (dashed for a room, solid for a volume; a square for a box, a circle for a cylinder, a squircle for a free-form, from 06). Click selects (`Shift` or `Cmd/Ctrl` toggles), double-click renames, dragging a row drops it before, after or into a group, and hovering a row highlights it in the view (from 03). From 07B each row has an **eye** (hide), a **lock** and an **isolate** button (on hover, or while on), and rows that don't show in the view are dimmed.
- **profile knobs**: small spheres on a single selected closed shape, part of the transform gizmo (from 07B): a room's **wall knob** (brick) on its wall's inner face halfway up, and a volume's or hole's **taper knob** (teal) on its top and **bevel knob** (pink) on its side where the rounding starts, on opposite sides between an edge's midpoint and a corner (clear of the scale handles). Dragging the taper knob toward the center tapers, the bevel knob down the side rounds (no bevel knob once the top is a point), the wall knob across the wall thickens (up to what leaves an inside). They snap to 0.05 (`Cmd/Ctrl` for free), preview live with a `taper 0.40` label, and each drag is one step. They work on tilted shapes, in the shape's own frame.
- **pen tool**: `P`, draws a free-form: click for a corner point, click and drag for a smooth one (the drag pulls out its handles, not snapped), click the first point or press `Enter` to close, `Backspace` removes the last point, `Esc` drops the outline. Points snap to 0.5 m (`Cmd/Ctrl` no snap). The preview turns red when the path crosses itself, and a closing outline that would is refused with a notice. The outline survives Space-panning (from 06). From 07B its first point sets the surface the free-form stands on (see **drawing on surfaces**).
- **project bar**: `Castle ▸ Entrance`, the button at the top left, above the outliner, as wide as the names need (at least the outliner's width, at most 640 px): the open project and scene. Clicking it opens the project picker (from 04; its own button from 05, first in the info-label before).
- **project picker**: the modal for projects and scenes: projects on the left (double-click renames, **New project** shows the create project form), and the selected project's description and scenes on the right. Clicking a scene opens it for every tab and the agent. Each scene row has rename and duplicate buttons on hover, and a duplicate copies the history and opens the copy. With nothing open it can't be dismissed, and with no projects at all it's only the **create project** form (name, description, first scene) (from 04).
- **repeat copy (`Alt+J`)**: copies the current selection by the last Alt-drag copy's offset and selects the copies, so pressing it again continues a row. The offset is remembered per tab and forgotten when the scene changes. With nothing to repeat, the info-label says "Alt-drag a copy first" (from 05).
- **ramp**: a shape that's a path with a width, walked along its top: a ramp, stairs, a landing, a walkway or a spiral stair (`type: "ramp"`, ID `ramp_N`). Its `points` are the centerline in absolute world x/z, each with the surface's height `y` there, and flat handles `in` / `out` curve it. Between two points the height changes evenly with the distance along the ground, so two points at the same y make a landing. `step` makes it stairs: each edge gets round(rise / step) equal steps, the top one flush with the higher end. `base` is `solid` (filled down to just below its lowest point) or `floating` (a 0.2 m slab under its surface). Always a volume (never a room, and never a hole since 07B), with no x, z, y, height or rotation: moving, turning and mirroring change its points. A turn too tight for its width is refused. The agent can give a `spiral` instead of points. Drawn with the **ramp tool** (from 07).
- **ramp tool**: `R`, draws a ramp like the Line tool draws a line: each click places a point on the surface under the cursor (so the floor, then a platform's top, is a ramp in two clicks), click-drag curves it, and a double-click or `Enter` finishes. The inspector sets the width, smooth or the step height, and solid or floating, the contextual bar the color, and the preview is a draft of the ramp. Selected ramps get the same controls plus **Reverse**. Double-click a ramp to edit its points, as a line's (from 07).
- **rotate handle**: the purple, nearly closed ring just outside the (-x, -z) top corner of the **selection frame**. Dragging it rotates the selection around the frame's center, with 15° snaps of the frame's angle (`Cmd/Ctrl` for free) and a live angle label (from 03; turning around the selection frame from 06).
- **scale handles**: the 8 small white squares on the corners and edge midpoints of a single selected box's top face, turned with the box. Dragging one resizes the box in its own axes with the opposite side fixed: `Shift` keeps the aspect ratio, `Alt` scales from the center, `Cmd/Ctrl` turns off snap (from 03) A tilted shape's sit on its own tilted top face and keep the opposite side fixed in the world, and its height handle sits on that face's center and keeps its bottom fixed (from 07B).
- **selection frame**: the rectangle the gizmo sits on. For a single box or cylinder it's the shape's own footprint rectangle. For anything else it's the frame around the selection, which turns as you rotate it and stays turned while the same selection stays selected (Figma-style). Its center is the rotate pivot, so rotating back lands where you started. An undo or someone else's change resets it to axis-aligned (`selectionFrame` in `geometry.ts`) (from 06).
- **select tool**: `V`, click to select (`Shift`-click adds or removes), drag a box to move the selection, drag empty ground for a marquee, `Cmd/Ctrl+A` selects all. It pans only with `Space` or the middle button (from 03).
- **selection**: the nodes currently selected in the editor, highlighted with a blue tint and outline (a group highlights all its boxes; hovering what a click would select tints it yellow) and described in the inspector. The Select tool selects on click (the outermost group, or inside an **entered group** its child), `Esc` deselects, `Delete` removes the selection, and drawing never changes it (from 03). It's also reported to the server as **selection (scene)**.
- **sides control**: in the inspector for the Cylinder tool and a selected cylinder: a smooth toggle (a circle icon), then − / the count / +. From smooth, − or + switch to the last count used (8 at first). Typing a count and pressing Enter sets it, clamped to 3..64 (from 06).
- **tool-bar**: the floating bar at the bottom center, with a margin from the window edges and the info-label. It holds only controls: the big icon tool buttons (Select, Hand, Box, with the shortcut letter in the corner), then Undo, Redo and Clear icon buttons. Counts and hints live in the info-label (from 03).
- **tilt (`pitch`, `roll`)**: a box or cylinder volume's turn around its own x axis (`pitch`, + leans its top toward its local +z) and its own z axis (`roll`, + toward its local -x), in degrees through its center, roll first, then pitch, then `rotation` (so turning it never changes its tilt). Stored in -180..180 and left out at 0. `y` stays the bottom before tilting, and `height` the length along the tilted axis: a cylinder lying on its side is pitch 90. `get_scene` gives a tilted shape its real `bounds`. Mirroring flips the pitch (on Z) or the roll (on X). Free-forms and rooms don't tilt, and a tilted shape can't convert. Set with the **tilt rings** or the inspector's pitch and roll fields, and **Reset tilt** levels it (from 07).
- **tilt rings**: two rings of the transform gizmo on a single box or cylinder volume, around its center: red for pitch (square to its own x axis, which stays level) and blue for roll (square to its own z axis, as tilted by the pitch). Dragging one turns the shape around that axis, snapping to 15° (`Cmd/Ctrl` for free), with a `pitch 30°` label. While a shape is tilted, its scale and height handles hide (from 07).
- **transform gizmo**: the handles on the selection, in the Select tool only: body drag and x/z arrows to move, a y arrow to raise, 8 footprint handles and a height handle to scale, a rotate handle (from 03). Replaces the height gizmo.
- **yaw**: rotation of the camera around the vertical axis through the focus point. It's the only rotation the camera has (A/D or ←/→).

## Scene and data

- **actor**: who made a change, either `human` (the editor) or `agent` (MCP). Stored on each box as `createdBy`. Not shown in the view since the graybox look.
- **bevel**: a volume's `bevel`, a fraction from 0 (a sharp top edge, left out of the data) to 1 (as round as it fits): the top edge rounds along a quarter circle of radius bevel × min(height, the top's half smallest extent). A tall smooth cylinder at 1 gets a dome. The bottom stays flat. Set with the inspector's bevel slider, which previews while dragged (from 07).
- **box**: a footprint plus a height: `{ id, type: "box", name?, kind, x, z, y, width, depth, height, rotation, color, createdBy }` in meters. `x, z` is the footprint's **center** (from 03, it was the min corner in 02), `width` and `depth` run along the box's own axes, and it rises from `y` to `y + height`. Its kind is either **room** or **volume** and can change. Its ID is `box_N`, never reused (from 03, it was per kind in 02).
- **command**: one user-level edit and one undo step. It's made of ops, records its actor, and stores its inverse (from 02).
- **compass**: the fixed directions of every scene: north is -z, south +z, east +x, west -x (y up). At yaw 0 the camera looks north. `get_scene` reports it as `compass`, and the server instructions state it, so "the north wall" means the same to the human and the agent (from 06).
- **copy (duplicate)**: a node copied with its whole subtree: new IDs, the same names, structure and parent group, offset by `dx, dy, dz` (`count` copies make a row, copy i offset i times). It goes right after its original in the list, and every copy call is one step (`Copy group_1 ×3`). The store's `duplicateNodes`, the `duplicate_nodes` message, and `move_nodes` with `copy: true` for the agent (from 05).
- **cylinder**: a shape with exactly a box's fields plus an optional `sides`. Its footprint is the ellipse inscribed in its width × depth rectangle (an oval when they differ), turned by `rotation`. With `sides` (3..64) it's a regular polygon on that ellipse, with a flat edge facing its local +x (an octagon has flat walls facing the axes). Without, it's smooth (drawn with 64 segments, lit as round). A room or a volume like a box. Mirroring an odd-sided one on X sets `180 − rotation`. ID `cylinder_N` (from 06).
- **data folder**: where the server saves everything: `./data` where it runs (gitignored), or `DATA_DIR`. It holds `app.json` (the last open scene), `projects/`, and a `.lock` so only one server uses it (from 04).
- **editor state**: a scene's `editor.json`: the camera (focus, yaw, zoom distance) and the selection. It's written 500 ms after they stop changing, and right away before switching scenes or stopping the server. It's restored when the scene opens. It isn't design data and isn't undoable (from 04).
- **elevation (`y`)**: the height of a box's bottom above the ground, in meters. 0 = on the ground; negative is below ground (from 03).
- **footprint**: a closed shape's outline on the ground, as a polygon in world x/z (`footprint()` in `geometry.ts`; a box's is its 4 corners). Its meshes and room walls are built from it, so a new shape type only adds its footprint and its move / rotate / mirror cases (from 06). For a room it's the outside of its walls, which grow inward from it (from 07; the wall centerline before).
- **free-form**: a shape whose footprint is a closed outline of **points** in absolute world x/z (3 to 500; the last joins the first), each with optional bezier handles `in` / `out` as offsets from it (a point without them is a corner). It has `y`, `height`, `kind` and `color`, but no center, size or rotation: moving, rotating, mirroring and scaling change its points (a mirror keeps their order). Its outline may not cross itself. Room walls are the outline offset ±0.1 m with Clipper2, so concave outlines get clean walls. ID `freeform_N` (from 06).
- **group**: a node that contains other nodes (via their `parent`), with ID `group_N`. It has no position of its own: its bounds come from its boxes (`get_scene` reports them as `bounds`), and moving, rotating or deleting it acts on everything inside as one command. A group left empty disappears in the same command (from 03).
- **handle frame**: a shape's own rectangle: a box's or cylinder's footprint rectangle (turned with it), a free-form's world-axis bounds (`handleFrame` in `geometry.ts`). The gizmo sits on the **selection frame**, which for a single box or cylinder is this (from 06).
- **hole**: the third kind of closed shape (`kind: "hole"`, next to room and volume): any box, cylinder or free-form that cuts itself out of other shapes when drawn (a door, a window, an arch, a hole in a floor). For a hole in group G, whose parent is P (the top level when G is top-level), it cuts the shapes directly in G, directly in P (beside G), and directly in G's and P's sibling groups, so a `door` group next to a room's walls cuts them and the walls of the rooms beside that room (the rule since 07B). It never cuts holes or lines, and only cuts what it overlaps. A hole outside any group cuts nothing, and shows a yellow warning diamond in the view and a warning in the outliner. It cuts a room's floor only if it reaches below the room's `y`. Default height 2.2 m, and it can taper, bevel and tilt. Drawn as a translucent ghost with dashed edges, and picked like a solid shape. The cut happens only when drawing (manifold, in the browser): the data stays plain shapes (`holes.ts`, `csg.ts`) (from 07).
- **hide (`hidden`)**: a node marked `hidden: true` (from 07B, with the outliner row's eye or `update_nodes`): it and everything in it aren't drawn, so they can't be picked, drawn on or snapped to, and a hidden hole cuts nothing. The outliner still lists and selects it (dimmed, with the gizmo on it). Hiding and showing are undo steps (`Hide box_3`, `Show box_3`) and are saved with the scene. A human's aid, like **lock**; the agent sees `hidden` and knows the node still exists.
- **history**: the single linear list of commands shared by the human and the agent. Undo reverts the latest command, whoever made it (from 02) From 04 it's per scene and survives restarts, rebuilt from the **history log**.
- **history log**: a scene's `history.jsonl`, append-only, one line per step (`commit` with its ops and inverse, `undo`, `redo`), numbered by `seq`. `scene.json` records the `seq` it includes. The log is written first, so after a crash it can be one step ahead, and opening the scene applies that step (from 04).
- **line**: an annotation shape: an open path of 2 or more points in absolute world x/y/z (each point has its own y), with optional 3D bezier handles, a palette `color` (black by default), a `thickness` in screen pixels (1..12, constant at any zoom), `dashed` and `arrow` (none, end or both). It has no kind, elevation or height. It's drawn over the shapes (fainter behind them), casts no shadow, and is picked within a few pixels of its path. Selected, it keeps its color and gets a blue halo with rings on its points (hovered: a yellow halo). Moving, rotating and mirroring change its points, and **Reverse** flips its direction. Point editing works on it, with a y arrow on the selected point. ID `line_N` (from 06).
- **mesh (shape mesh)**: a closed shape as triangles, built in `mesh.ts`: `shapeMesh` gives the parts it's drawn with in its own frame (a volume's solid, or a room's walls and floor slab), and `hitMesh` what picking and the marquee test in world space (the body, plus a room's floor at its elevation). Meshes are watertight and share vertices (from 07).
- **mirror**: flipping nodes in place on a world axis, X (east ↔ west) or Z, across the center of their combined footprint bounds. Each box's center reflects and its rotation becomes -rotation, and `y` never changes. A group mirrors as a unit, and mirroring twice restores the exact values. `Shift+X` / `Shift+Z`, the mirror buttons in the inspector's Actions (a flip icon, turned a quarter for Z, with a red X or blue Z in the corner), and the `mirror_nodes` tool. Mirroring only works in place: to mirror a copy, Alt-drag it first (from 05).
- **name**: an optional human-friendly label on any node ("lobby"). Not unique. Tools still take IDs (from 03).
- **node**: anything in the scene's flat list: a box or a group. Each has an `id`, an optional `name` and an optional `parent` (from 03).
- **op**: the smallest reversible scene change (`add`, `remove`, `update`, and `order` for the outliner's reordering) inside a command.
- **palette**: the fixed set of color keys a box can use: the neutrals `white` (#ffffff), `almost-white` (#ededed, the default) and `gray` (#c4c4c4), plus pastels (`blue`, `yellow`, ...). The keys are the contract, and the pastel hex values are tuned freely (from 03).
- **room**: a hollow closed shape: a floor slab and walls, no ceiling. Default height 3 m. Its footprint is its outside, and its walls grow inward from it (see **wall thickness**).
- **rotation**: a box's turn around the vertical axis through its center, in degrees, counterclockwise seen from above. 0 = grid-aligned (from 03).
- **open scene**: the one scene the server has open, shared by every tab and the agent, or none. Undo acts within it (from 04).
- **project**: a folder of scenes for one game, with a name and a description the agent reads. Its ID is a slug of its name at creation, and it holds `scenes/` plus the empty semantic folders `abilities/`, `entities/` and `rules/` (from 04).
- **scene**: the full design state the server owns: the `view`, the `selection` and all nodes (`scene.nodes`, from 03). The editor and the agent both read and edit the same scene. From 04 it's one scene (one level) of a project, saved on disk in `scenes/<id>/scene.json` after every step, and its ID is a slug of its name at creation.
- **selection (scene)**: the IDs of the selected nodes, reported in `scene.selection` so the agent knows what "this" means. Last tab wins, not undoable (from 03).
- **shape**: any node that isn't a group: a box, a cylinder, a free-form, a line or a ramp (from 07). The first three are **closed shapes** (a footprint, a kind, an elevation and a height; `ClosedShape` / `isClosed` in the code); a line has none of those. Closed shapes and ramps are **solids** (`Solid` / `isSolid`: a kind and a mesh, so holes cut them; only closed shapes can be holes). Each type has its own ID counter (`box_N`, `cylinder_N`, `freeform_N`, `line_N`). The agent draws them with `draw_shapes`, each with a `type` (from 06).
- **snap**: rounding a drawn footprint to the nearest 0.5 m. On by default, and holding `Cmd/Ctrl` while drawing turns it off (from 03; `Alt` in 02).
- **snap (vertical)**: rounding heights to the nearest 0.05 m. It's also the minimum height (from 02).
- **taper**: a volume's `taper`, a fraction from 0 (straight sides, left out of the data) to 1 (the top is a point): the top is the outline scaled by 1 − taper toward its center, so a box at 1 is a pyramid and a cylinder a cone. A free-form scales toward its outline's centroid. Set with the inspector's taper slider, which previews while dragged. Making a volume a room drops its taper and bevel (from 07).
- **view**: what the editor currently shows: the focus point, the yaw and `bounds`, the axis-aligned box around the visible ground. The editor reports it to the server so the agent knows where to draw. From 07B it also carries `isolated`, the node the human has isolated, if any.
- **view bar**: the small bar at the top right (from 07B), next to the error panel: the **holes** toggle (off hides the hole ghosts, so only what they cut away shows, and hidden holes can't be clicked unless selected; it was the status bar's **Show holes** in 07), the **grid** toggle, and while a node is isolated its name with ✕. For clean views and images; for this tab only.
- **volume**: a solid box, something you stand on or bump into. Default height 0.25 m.
- **wall thickness (`wall`)**: a room's walls' thickness in meters, 0.2 by default (left out of the data then) and at least 0.05, grown **inward** from the footprint, so the footprint is the room's outside. Two rooms that touch have two walls back to back; overlap them by a wall's thickness to share one. A room too narrow for an inside is drawn solid. Set in the inspector's wall thickness field (for selected rooms, and the next room in the drawing tools). A room that becomes a volume drops it (from 07; before, every wall was 0.2 m, centered on the footprint edge).
- **world unit (u)**: the coordinate unit of the scene. It's abstract for now and becomes meters in 02 (1 u = 1 m). At default zoom 1 u = 20 px. The data never stores pixels.

## System

- **MCP server / tools**: the `/mcp` endpoint Claude Code connects to. Current tools: `get_scene`, `draw_shapes` (was `draw_boxes` until 06), `update_nodes`, `remove_nodes`, `move_nodes` (with `copy`, from 05), `rotate_nodes`, `mirror_nodes` (from 05), `group_nodes`, `ungroup`.
- **server instructions**: the conventions text (units, boxes, groups, IDs, the view, copying) the MCP server sends once when a client connects, instead of repeating it in every tool description (from 05).
- **server**: the single local process (`npm run dev`) that owns the open scene, saves it in the data folder, and serves the editor, the WebSocket (`/ws`) and MCP (`/mcp`).
